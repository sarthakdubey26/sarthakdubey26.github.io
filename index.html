Research

SNGR: Selective Non-Gaussian Refinement for Ambiguous SLAM Factor Graphs

Preprint on arXiv
PaperPythonGTSAMiSAM2Factor graphsNested sampling
Read paper
Details
  • Augmented iSAM2 with targeted nested sampling to refine the regions of a SLAM factor graph where Gaussian posterior assumptions fail.
  • Built a failure-detection trigger from condition numbers of joint marginal covariances, flagging unreliable regions before running expensive refinement.
  • Refined locally with the full nonlinear likelihood, with a gate that blocks updates that would degrade multimodal cases.
  • On range-only SLAM with wrong data associations: high-precision failure detection and consistent likelihood gains at lower cost than exhaustive non-Gaussian inference.

Localization and perception

Multi-Sensor Fusion for Robot Localization

Drift cut from 45.8% to 3.1%
ROS2C++PythonEKFVisual odometryGPS/IMU
Project report
Details
  • ROS2 localization pipeline fusing u-blox GPS, IMU and a monocular camera, with timestamp sync and TF transforms across indoor and outdoor runs.
  • IMU-aided visual odometry with quaternion orientation correction, reducing cumulative trajectory drift from 45.8% to 3.1%.
  • Benchmarked complementary filter vs. EKF using covariance and innovation-residual analysis under sparse, intermittent GPS.
  • SVD-based Procrustes alignment and scale correction to recover accuracy after sensor dropouts and estimator resets.
